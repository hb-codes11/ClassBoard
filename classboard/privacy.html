Privacy & Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Effective immediately for all visitors, students, and contributors

At ClassBoard (accessible from classboardx.in, its subdomains, and associated web applications), the privacy, security, and confidentiality of our student community is an absolute priority. This Privacy Policy details our operational practices regarding the collection, processing, protection, and retention of personal data, in strict accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000 (and 2021 Intermediary Guidelines), and global digital advertising transparency standards.

By creating an account, authenticating through institutional or Google login, or accessing ClassBoard services, you consent to the data collection and processing practices described herein. If you do not consent, please refrain from using the platform.

1. Data Controller & Contact Information

For the purposes of data governance and applicable Indian privacy laws, ClassBoard is managed by its founding engineering developers:

  • Data Fiduciaries: Hasnaat Malik & Harsh Bhadrawale
  • Platform: ClassBoard (classboardx.in)
  • Official Inquiries & Privacy Desk: contact@classboardx.in
  • Jurisdiction: Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India

2. Categories of Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to deliver a streamlined academic coordination experience:

A. Student Identity & Profile Information

  • Institutional Authentication: Email address (e.g., student@medicaps.ac.in) and verified Google profile name provided during authentication via Google OAuth.
  • Academic Profile: Enrolled branch section (e.g., INTEL-1, IBM-1), student serial number, and designated practical laboratory batch (e.g., A1, A2, B1, B2).
  • Role & Permissions: Designations assigned to manage classroom logistics (e.g., Student, Class Representative / CR, Section Admin, Founder).

B. Academic Planning & Self-Service Records

  • Personal Attendance Checklists: Student-recorded attended classes and subject tracking checkboxes saved to calculate individualized 75% attendance projections.
  • Academic Grievances & Concerns: Attendance disputes, subject-specific remarks, or scheduling issues submitted to Class Representatives through the "Attendance Concerns" channel.
  • Course Resource Interactions: Subject notes bookmarked, syllabus references accessed, and exam preparation resources viewed.

C. Technical & Operational Telemetry Data

  • Device & Connection Data: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser family and version, operating system, device viewport dimensions, and referrer URLs.
  • Platform Route Telemetry: Anonymized timestamps of page views, active tab navigations, and session duration to assess server capacity and prevent platform outages during peak morning attendance logging hours.
Zero Commercial Sale Guarantee: We strictly pledge that ClassBoard has never sold, rented, leased, or monetized student personal data, email addresses, or academic records to third-party data brokers, commercial coaching institutes, or marketing agencies, and will never do so.

3. Purposes & Lawful Basis of Processing

We process your data based on legitimate student educational interests, user consent, and platform contract execution for the following specific purposes:

  1. Academic Utility Delivery: Displaying accurate daily timetables, held class tallies, examination notices, and personalized safe-bunk estimates based on your assigned section and lab batch.
  2. Strict Section Isolation & Privacy: Enforcing access controls so students belonging to Intel (AI & Analytics) can only view Intel resources and students in IBM (Advanced AI) view IBM records.
  3. Peer Representation: Enabling elected Class Representatives to review and address student attendance concerns directly without exposing sensitive student contact info.
  4. Platform Security & Abuse Prevention: Guarding against brute-force attacks, automated web scraping bots, and credential forgery.

4. Backend Infrastructure & Row Level Security (RLS)

Your academic records and profiles are protected by multi-layered cloud security infrastructure:

  • Database Hosting & Encryption: Our database is hosted on Supabase Inc. enterprise cloud servers. All communications between your browser and our servers are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 (Transport Layer Security) with high-grade HTTPS encryption.
  • Row Level Security (RLS) Enforcement: All database tables (including profiles, member roles, attendance logs, and grievance submissions) enforce strict PostgreSQL Row Level Security. Even within the database layer, an unauthenticated user or student from an unauthorized branch cannot query or extract private serial numbers, attendance checklists, or peer profiles of other cohorts.

5. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies Disclosure

AdSense Publisher Compliance Notice: ClassBoard displays non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdSense to subsidize cloud database bandwidth, compute resources, and software upkeep. Because Google serves ads on our site, we comply with all Google AdSense publisher transparency guidelines:
  • Third-Party Vendor Cookies: Google and its certified advertising partners use cookies (such as the Google DoubleClick cookie) to serve advertisements based on a user's prior visits to ClassBoard or other websites across the Internet.
  • Personalized Advertising Opt-Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page.
  • Network Advertising Opt-Out: Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info or the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) opt-out tool.

6. Cookies & Local Storage Usage

ClassBoard utilizes the following client-side technologies to maintain portal functionality:

  • Essential Session Storage: Temporary browser storage maintaining your encrypted Supabase OAuth session token, preventing you from having to log in repeatedly during active campus hours.
  • Local Preferences Cache: HTML5 LocalStorage used to store local device preferences, including dark mode styling, active section tabs, offline PWA cache, and collapsed notification banners. LocalStorage resides exclusively on your local device and is never transmitted to advertisers.
  • Advertising Cookies: Managed directly by Google AdSense as outlined in Section 5 above.

7. Data Retention & Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:

  • Semester Lifecycle Retention: Routine academic logs, attendance records, and section rosters are retained for the duration of the active academic year. Historical logs may be archived or reset at the start of each new academic session.
  • Account Erasure: Any student who transfers, graduates, or wishes to discontinue use of ClassBoard may request complete, permanent erasure of their account profile, serial number mapping, and submitted records by emailing our data team at contact@classboardx.in. All associated records will be purged from our active databases within 14 business days.

8. Children & Minor Privacy

ClassBoard is designed exclusively for university undergraduates, postgraduates, and faculty members (individuals aged 17 and above). We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or store personal information from children under 13 years of age. If we learn that personal data of a minor under 13 has been collected without parental consent, we will promptly delete it.

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect technical modifications, feature additions, or legislative requirements. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued usage of ClassBoard following notification of modifications signifies your agreement to the revised policy.
