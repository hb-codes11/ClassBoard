Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Effective immediately

At ClassBoard (accessible from our official web domain and web application), student and user privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected, how it is used, and the choices you have regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you use ClassBoard, we collect certain information to provide an accurate and secure educational experience:

  • Account Information: College email address (e.g. @medicaps.ac.in), student display name, enrollment/class section, and roll number provided during registration or authentication.
  • Academic Preferences & Planner Records: Attendance entries, calculated projections, bookmarked subject notes, and student concerns submitted to Class Representatives.
  • Log & Telemetry Data: Browser user agent, device type, connection timestamps, and activity events (e.g., viewing notes, checking the exam schedule) to ensure portal security and uptime.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly utilized to:

  • Provide, maintain, and personalize the ClassBoard portal experience.
  • Calculate attendance safety projections and notify students about timetable changes and MST schedules.
  • Verify authorized student and section administrator status.
  • Detect and prevent unauthorized cross-section access or security vulnerabilities.
Student Data Guarantee: We do not sell, rent, or trade student email addresses or private academic data to third parties under any circumstances.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

ClassBoard may partner with third-party advertising vendors, including Google AdSense, to display relevant advertisements to support ongoing hosting and server infrastructure costs.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on ClassBoard.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you may opt out of third-party vendor cookies by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Log Files and Web Beacons

Like standard web applications, ClassBoard utilizes standard log files. The information inside the log files includes Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to personally identifiable information and are used solely for site administration and traffic trend analysis.

5. Data Security

We employ enterprise-grade security protocols, including Row Level Security (RLS) on our backend databases, encrypted SSL/TLS connections, and multi-layered role authorization (Student, Class Representative, Admin, Founder) to protect your personal data from unauthorized access.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Every student and user is entitled to:

  • Request access to the personal data recorded under their profile.
  • Request correction of any incorrect or outdated academic information.
  • Request deletion of their account records upon leaving the institution.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our Contact Us page or email our development team directly.
